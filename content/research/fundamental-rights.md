# Fundamental Rights under the Constitution of India

## Introduction

Fundamental Rights are the basic rights guaranteed to individuals under Part III of the Constitution of India. These rights protect personal liberty, equality and dignity of citizens.

## Constitutional Framework

The Fundamental Rights are mentioned under Articles 12 to 35 of the Constitution of India.

These rights include:

- Right to Equality (Articles 14-18)
- Right to Freedom (Articles 19-22)
- Right against Exploitation (Articles 23-24)
- Right to Freedom of Religion (Articles 25-28)
- Cultural and Educational Rights (Articles 29-30)
- Right to Constitutional Remedies (Article 32)

## Landmark Judgments

### Kesavananda Bharati v. State of Kerala

The Supreme Court established the Basic Structure Doctrine and held that Parliament cannot alter the basic structure of the Constitution.

### Maneka Gandhi v. Union of India

The Supreme Court expanded the interpretation of Article 21 and recognized the importance of fair procedure and personal liberty.

## Conclusion

Fundamental Rights form the foundation of Indian constitutional democracy and protect individuals against arbitrary state action.
